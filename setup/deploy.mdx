Work through this checklist before you put the agent in front of real users. Most of it is dashboard configuration. Saving agent and tool settings requires an organization admin.

## Before you launch

### Installation

- **Website is set.** In **Settings** → **Products**, the product's **Website** is your production domain. The widget only loads on that domain and its subdomains, except on shared hosting domains such as `vercel.app`, where only the exact host and its `www.` form match. See [Install the widget](/setup/embed-widget#before-you-start).
- **The snippet is on every page that needs it.** Check your page templates. Your production domain is listed under **Verify installation** on **Deploy** → **Install**, but a listed domain only shows the script ran there, not that the widget rendered.
- **The mode is right.** **Regular** for a floating chat bubble, or **Copilot** for a sidebar, chosen in **Deploy** → **Install** or **Agent** → **Mode**. Remove any `data-mode` attribute you used for testing.

### Agent

- **Identity and instructions.** **Agent Name** and **Welcome Message** are set in **Agent** → **Identity**, and the **System Prompt** in **Agent** → **Instructions** describes your product and tone. See [Identity and instructions](/agent/identity-instructions).
- **Design.** The widget matches your brand. See [Mode and design](/agent/mode-design).
- **Suggestions.** The starter chips cover your most common questions. See [User suggestions](/agent/user-suggestions).
- **Escalation.** You've decided what happens when a user asks for a person. See [Escalation](/conversations/escalation).

### Knowledge

- **Sources are in.** Your help center, docs, and key files are in **Knowledge Base**, and file uploads show **Completed**. See [Knowledge base](/knowledge-base/knowledge-base).
- **Answers are right.** You've asked the agent the questions your users ask most, and it answers them correctly.

### Tools

- **Routes are reviewed.** In **Tools** → **Page Navigation**, you've reviewed discovered routes and clicked **Save routes**. Discovered routes don't go live until you do. See [Page navigation](/agent/page-navigation).
- **Workflows are tested.** Every workflow in **Tools** → **Workflows** runs end to end on your production site. See [Workflows](/agent/workflows).
- **In-page actions are set the way you want.** In **Tools** → **In-Page Actions**, decide whether to keep **Require approval before actions** on. See [In-page actions](/agent/in-page-actions).
- **APIs are connected, if you use them.** Your OpenAPI spec is uploaded in **Tools** → **Server Side**, auth is configured, and only the endpoints you want are enabled. See [API integration](/integration/api-integration).

### Users

- **Users are identified.** Signed-in users' attributes reach Argide through the script tag or `update`. See [User identification](/setup/user-identification).
- **Logout clears the identity.** Your logout code calls `window.argide('resetAndClearSession')`.
- **Signed-in API calls work, if you use them.** Your backend signs JWTs with the **ARGIDE_AUTH_SECRET** from **Deploy** → **Backend Integration**, and your frontend calls `identify` on login and page load. Refresh the token before it expires. The snippet on that page calls `identify` every 50 minutes. See [Identity verification](/integration/identity-verification).

<Warning>
  Keep **ARGIDE_AUTH_SECRET** on your server. Anyone who has it can sign tokens that impersonate your users.
</Warning>

### Team

- **Teammates are invited.** Everyone who answers conversations is a member. See [Members](/organization/members).
- **Follow-up is set up.** Turn on the rating survey in **Settings** → **Satisfaction**. Email notifications for unseen replies are on by default; check them in **Settings** → **Email**. See [Satisfaction](/conversations/satisfaction) and [Email notifications](/conversations/email-notifications).

## Test in production

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your site">
    Load a page on your production domain. The chat bubble appears in the bottom corner, or the copilot sidebar opens beside the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test as a signed-out visitor">
    Ask a few general questions. Check that the answers come from your knowledge base.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test as a signed-in user">
    Sign in and start a conversation. In **Conversations**, check that it shows your name instead of **Anonymous visitor**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try navigation and workflows">
    Ask the agent to take you to a page, and to do a task you've built a workflow for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try your API tools">
    If you connected an API, ask for something that needs it, such as an order status. Confirm the result matches your system.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask for a person">
    Check that the conversation reaches your team the way you configured escalation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign out">
    Log out and open the widget again. The previous conversation is gone, and new chats are anonymous.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Watch it after launch

Every conversation lands in **Conversations**:

- **All conversations**, **Resolved**, **Escalated & Handoff**, and **Pending** split conversations by outcome.
- Open any conversation to read it, reply to the user, or leave an internal note for your team.
- **Satisfaction** reports ratings, response rate, and comments once the survey is on.

See [Conversations](/conversations/inbox) for the full tour. When the agent gets something wrong, fix the source: add knowledge, adjust the instructions, or refine the workflow.
