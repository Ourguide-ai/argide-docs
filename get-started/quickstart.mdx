---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "Put a working Argide agent on your site in a few minutes"
---

Set your product's Website, give the agent something to know, and paste one snippet into your site.

<Warning>
  **The widget only loads on your product's Website domain and its subdomains.** Until you set a Website, it loads nowhere. There is no exception for `localhost`, so test on a page served from your Website's domain.
</Warning>

## Before you start

You need:

- An Argide account. Sign in to the [dashboard](https://dashboard.argide.ai/dashboard).
- Admin access to your organization. Only organization admins can create or edit products.
- A site where you can add a `<script>` tag.

If you completed the onboarding flow when you signed up, you already entered your website there. Check it in step 1 anyway.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set your Website">
    Your product's Website decides where the widget is allowed to load.

    1. Open **Settings** → **Products**.
    2. Find your product. To start a new one, click **New Product** and fill in **Product Name** and **Website URL**.
    3. To change an existing product, click its edit icon, update **Website URL**, and click **Save**.

    Check that the product card shows the right **Website**. Enter the full URL, such as `https://example.com`. A Website of `https://example.com` also covers `www.example.com` and `app.example.com`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add knowledge">
    Open **Knowledge Base** and give the agent something to answer from:

    - Under **Help center & docs**, select **Public site**, enter your help center or docs URL, and click **Ingest Content**.
    - Under **Files**, drop in documents such as PDF, DOCX, XLSX, TXT, Markdown, CSV, or JSON.

    You can skip this to see the widget first, but the agent has little to say until it has knowledge. [Knowledge base](/knowledge-base/knowledge-base) covers each source.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the snippet">
    Open **Deploy** → **Install**. Under **Add the script**, click **Copy**. The snippet already contains your Product ID:

    ```html
    <script>window.argide = window.argide || [];</script>
    <script
      src="https://dashboard.argide.ai/argide-b2b.iife.js"
      data-product-id="YOUR_PRODUCT_ID"
      data-api-url="https://api.argide.ai"
    ></script>
    ```

    Under **Choose how it appears**, pick **Regular** for a floating chat bubble or **Copilot** for a sidebar. The choice saves immediately, and you can change it later without touching the snippet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it into your site">
    Paste the snippet into your site's HTML, just before the closing `</body>` tag, on every page where the agent should appear. Deploy the change to a domain covered by your Website.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the installation">
    Open your site in a browser. The chat bubble appears in the bottom corner, or the sidebar opens beside your page in copilot mode.

    Back in **Deploy** → **Install**, click **Refresh** under **Verify installation**. Your domain appears in the list once the widget has loaded there.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If the widget doesn't appear

- **Your domain shows under Verify installation, but no widget shows on the page.** The page's domain isn't your Website or one of its subdomains. Your browser's network tab shows a `403` for the Argide bundle. Fix the **Website URL** in **Settings** → **Products**.
- **You're testing on `localhost` or a preview URL.** Those don't match your Website. Test on your real domain or a subdomain of it.
- **Nothing shows under Verify installation.** Check that the snippet is on the page you opened and that `data-product-id` is set.

[Install the widget](/setup/embed-widget#troubleshooting) has the full troubleshooting list.
