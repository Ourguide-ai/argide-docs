Two pages shape who your agent is. **Agent** → **Identity** controls what users see in the chat header and the greeting. **Agent** → **Instructions** controls how the agent behaves and responds.

Both pages show a live preview of the widget on the right. Click **Save Changes** to apply your edits. The widget picks them up the next time a visitor loads your page.

<Note>
  You need the Admin role in your organization to save changes on any **Agent** page.
</Note>

## Identity

| Field | What it does | If left empty |
| ----- | ------------ | ------------- |
| **Agent Name** | Shown in the widget header. | The header reads "Assistant". |
| **Welcome Message** | The first message at the top of every conversation, shown as a message from the agent. Supports Markdown. | A built-in greeting. |

Both fields are display-only. The agent never sees them, so it won't know its own name unless you tell it in [Instructions](#instructions).

When a member of your team takes over a conversation from the inbox, the header changes to "Support Agent" for that conversation. See [Escalation](/conversations/escalation).

## Instructions

**System Prompt** is free text the agent reads on every turn of every conversation. Use it to set:

- **Persona and tone.** "You are Ava, the assistant for Acme Billing. Be brief and friendly. Never use emoji."
- **Scope.** "Only answer questions about invoices, payments, and subscriptions. For anything else, say you can't help with that here."
- **House rules for answers.** "Always link to the relevant help article when one exists."

Click the expand icon next to the field label to edit in a full-screen editor.

### What instructions can't do

Your instructions sit alongside Argide's built-in rules and can't override them. Instructions can't:

- Change how the agent uses its tools, such as [page navigation](/agent/page-navigation), [workflows](/agent/workflows), or [in-page actions](/agent/in-page-actions).
- Add steps or conditions to a workflow.
- Stop the agent from using a capability you've turned on, or make it claim a capability is unavailable.

The agent ignores anything in your instructions that tries to do these things and keeps your persona and tone. To turn a capability off, use its own page under **Tools** instead.

<Tip>
  Keep instructions about *how* to talk and *what* to cover. Put product facts in the [knowledge base](/knowledge-base/knowledge-base), where the agent searches them when it answers.
</Tip>
